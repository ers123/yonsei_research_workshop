# Argument Outline

Status: prepared showcase outline. This outline supports workshop drafting only; it does not authorize external circulation or publication-ready claims.

## Working Thesis

- Thesis: A persistent visual bar that links physical activity to screen-time availability can make behavioral consequences visible enough to support children's planning and perceived behavioral control, but incentive and penalty elements may also turn physical activity into a means of earning screen time rather than producing durable internalized motivation.
- Research question: How does a screen-time and physical-activity-linked visual bar interface shape children's perceived behavioral control and self-regulation of smartphone use?
- Output language: English
- Target audience: design/HCI-oriented peer-review audience; workshop walkthrough before venue selection
