# Benchmark Papers

Status: prepared showcase benchmarks. Benchmark papers guide structure and tone; they do not automatically authorize claims.

## Structure Patterns To Borrow

- Introduction pattern: Begin from child smartphone use and sedentary behavior, then move to limits of parental control and the need for self-regulation-supportive design.
- Literature review pattern: Connect behavior-change design, feedback/feedforward, incentives/penalties, and perceived behavioral control.
- Methods or materials pattern: Separate system design, participants, intervention period, behavioral data, interviews, and analysis decisions.
- Findings pattern: Present quantitative trends cautiously, then explain mechanisms through qualitative themes.
- Discussion pattern: Emphasize both potential and boundary conditions: legibility/planning, family mediation, incentive instrumentalization, technical reliability, and internalization limits.

## Patterns To Avoid

- Overly technical framing: Do not let app/patent details dominate the design-research contribution.
- Unsupported normative claims: Do not frame the intervention as better parenting or better child behavior.
- Methods language that does not fit this project: Do not claim randomized controlled trial or clinical evaluation unless confirmed.
- Other: Do not reuse pilot article claims as if they prove the current expanded dataset.
