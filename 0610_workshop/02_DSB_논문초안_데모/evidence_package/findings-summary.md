## Interpretation Boundaries

- Findings that can be stated directly: The materials support a workshop-level argument that the bar/signifier made behavior more visible and that parents/children interpreted the intervention through planning, reward, penalty, and negotiation.
- Findings that require cautious wording: Any statement that screen time decreased, physical activity increased, or perceived behavioral control improved must be phrased as provisional until statistical cleaning and measurement decisions are reviewed.
- Findings that should remain speculative: Internalization of motivation, sustained habit formation after the intervention, and generalization beyond this sample.
- Findings that should not be included: Participant-identifying anecdotes, raw quotations, clinical/health outcome claims, or claims that the system is proven effective.

## Method Notes

- Data source: Local reference corpus supplied by DSB Lab, including interview summaries/transcripts, survey responses, app/patent materials, pilot article, and screen-time/activity data.
- Collection period: Merged screen-time/activity CSV spans 2024-06-25 to 2025-01-16.
- Sample or corpus: 19 participants in merged quantitative file; interview materials appear to cover multiple rounds and participant families.
- Analysis method: For workshop draft, use sanitized thematic synthesis plus provisional aggregate pre/post data summary. For final manuscript, add deterministic data cleaning, statistical analysis, and human-coded qualitative evidence.
- Known limitations: Missing values and "not collected" values; uneven intervention windows; participant-level privacy; possible technical issues; translation risk; need to distinguish external reward response from self-regulation.
