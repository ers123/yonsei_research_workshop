## Copyright Boundary

This folder does not reproduce long third-party prompts, video transcripts, or slide text verbatim.

The prompts here are adapted workshop prompts written for research-writing practice. Short source phrases may be discussed orally in the workshop, but participants should consult the original source links for exact wording.

## Privacy Boundary

This public folder intentionally excludes:

- raw interview text;
- participant-level data;
- private file paths;
- private links;
- identifying filenames;
- unapproved quotes;
- unpublished raw analysis outputs.

## Research Responsibility Boundary

AI can help with:

- organizing materials;
- making outlines;
- checking claim-source consistency;
- drafting cautious prose;
- critiquing drafts;
- planning figures and tables;
- preparing revision logs.

AI must not be treated as final authority for:

- IRB/ethics decisions;
- privacy approval;
- direct quotation approval;
- statistical interpretation;
- causal/effectiveness claims;
- external circulation;
- publication-readiness decisions.
